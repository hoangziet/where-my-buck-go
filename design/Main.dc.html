Features

One conversation for all of your money.

No forms to fill in. Everything starts in the chat, and you can still edit any record by hand.

Talk, don't type forms

Write “coffee 35k, rent 6tr” the way you'd say it. The AI picks out amounts, dates and categories for you.

Snap a receipt

Attach a photo in the chat. The total, date, store and line items are read for you, ready to review.

Ask anything, get real numbers

Questions become SQL queries on your own data. Open the steps to see exactly what ran.

Nothing saved without you

Every AI suggestion arrives as a preview card. Confirm, edit or discard — your ledger stays clean.

A dashboard you build by asking

Pin any table or chart from the chat. Your dashboard is made of the questions you actually care about.

Undo and export anytime

Search, filter and fix records by hand, undo a delete in one tap, and export any result to CSV.
